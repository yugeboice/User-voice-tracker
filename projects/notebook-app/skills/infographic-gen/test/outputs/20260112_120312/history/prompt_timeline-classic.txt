**The Succession Crisis**

**The Impossible March**
Just three days after Stamford Bridge, William landed at Pevensey Bay with 7,000 Norman troops, forcing exhausted Harold to march his depleted army another 250 miles south in what became a fatal strategic disadvantage.

**Hastings: The Decisive Battle**
On October 14th at Senlac Hill, Harold's shield wall of elite housecarls with Danish axes initially held against Norman cavalry and archers, until rumors of William's death broke Anglo-Saxon formation and enabled the fatal counterattack.

**The Norman Revolution**
William's victory introduced French as the court language for 300 years, commissioned the comprehensive Domesday Book survey in 1086, and built the stone castles and cathedrals that still define England's landscape today.

**Birth of Anglo-Norman Civilization**
The conquest created a unique cultural fusion combining Norman-French governance, Anglo-Saxon traditions, and Latin scholarship that influenced medieval European development and established England's continental connections.
