**c) Key points - 5-7 most important concepts**

**Light Waves Create Colors**
Light travels in different sized waves called wavelengths. When objects absorb some wavelengths and reflect others back to our eyes, our brain translates these into colors we recognize.
Example: A red apple absorbs all colors except red wavelengths, which bounce back to make us see "red"

**Amazing Animal Vision** 
Butterflies see ultraviolet patterns on flowers that are completely invisible to humans, while bulls are actually colorblind and can't see red at all.
Example: The matador's cape movement, not its red color, makes bulls charge

**Rainbow Science Secrets**
Pink doesn't exist in rainbows - our brain creates it by mixing red and white light together. Rainbows show all the hidden colors that white light contains.
Example: Water in a glass can split sunlight into rainbow colors by bending different wavelengths

**Historical Color Facts**
Blue was so rare and expensive in ancient times that only royalty could afford it, giving us "royal blue." Ancient Egyptians only had words for six colors total.

**Colors Affect Our Feelings**
Red makes us feel energetic and excited, blue helps us feel calm, and green reminds us of nature while helping our eyes relax.
Example: Many classrooms use soft green or blue because these colors help students focus

**Simple Color Experiments**
Three easy home experiments show color science: rainbow in a glass with water and sunlight, color-dancing milk with soap, and spinning color wheels that blend into white.
